## 1. System Overview

**Applio** is a modern, modular, open-source voice conversion ecosystem built on the **Retrieval-based Voice Conversion (RVC v2)** neural architecture. It enables high-fidelity voice identity transfer while preserving linguistic timing and pronunciation.

In our project, Applio operates across two distinct pipelines:
1. **Pipeline 1 (Training Workflow):** Converts raw audio from our consenting team member (`teammate01_training.wav`) into trained generator weights (`teammate01_rvc.pth`) and a FAISS retrieval index (`teammate01_rvc.index`).
2. **Pipeline 2 (Inference Workflow):** Takes intermediate speech generated by Microsoft Edge-TTS and converts the generic voice into our team member's vocal timbre in real time.

## 3. Pipeline 1: The Applio Training Workflow (Step-by-Step)

```
[Target Audio: teammate01_training.wav]
                │
                ▼
      [1. Preprocessing & VAD Slicing]
      ├── Dynamic splitting into 2–3s slices
      ├── Resampling to 40,000 Hz (40 kHz)
      └── Peak amplitude normalization
                │
        ┌───────┴───────┐
        ▼               ▼
[2A. ContentVec]  [2B. RMVPE Pitch]
Extracts 256-dim  Extracts frame-level
phonetic vectors  F0 melody contour
        │               │
        └───────┬───────┘
                ▼
  [3. VITS Adversarial Training]
  • 100 Epochs (2,100 gradient steps)
  • Generator vs Multi-Scale Discriminator
  • Multi-task Loss (Adv + FeatureMatch + Mel + KL)
                │
        ┌───────┴────────────────────────┐
        ▼                                ▼
[Trained Weights (.pth)]       [4. FAISS Index Construction]
teammate01_rvc_100e_2100s.pth   Clusters all ContentVec latent
(VITS Generator backbone)       vectors into teammate01_rvc.index
```

### Step 1: Preprocessing & Dynamic Slicing
* **Input:** `teammate01_training.wav` (3.5 minutes, 44.1 kHz, mono uncompressed PCM).
* **Voice Activity Detection (VAD):** Deep neural networks cannot easily process a single continuous 3.5-minute audio tensor due to GPU memory constraints. Applio scans for natural pauses, breaths, and silent gaps, splitting the audio into **84 clean segments of 2 to 3 seconds each**.
* **Resampling to 40 kHz:** Standard speech is 16 kHz or 22.05 kHz. RVC v2 operates natively at **40,000 Hz** to reproduce crisp high frequencies up to 20 kHz.
* **Volume Normalization:** Peak amplitudes are scaled to $[-1.0, 1.0]$ to eliminate recording volume fluctuations.

### Step 2: Dual Feature Extraction (Content & Pitch)
For every sliced audio segment, Applio extracts two parallel streams:
1. **Semantic Content via ContentVec (HuBERT Backbone):**
   * Passes raw waveforms through 12 transformer encoder blocks.
   * Extracts **256-dimensional frame-level embeddings**.
   * *Purpose:* Captures the exact phonemes spoken without carrying the speaker's vocal identity (speaker-disentangled representation).
2. **Pitch Contour via RMVPE:**
   * Runs the **Robust Model for Vocal Pitch Estimation**.
   * Computes a continuous fundamental frequency ($F_0$) trajectory over time.
   * *Purpose:* Captures the intonation, inflection, and musicality of the speaker's voice.

### Step 3: VITS Adversarial Neural Training
Applio fine-tunes a pretrained 40k VITS (Variational Inference with adversarial learning for end-to-end Text-to-Speech) neural network:
* **The Generator ($G$):** Takes the ContentVec representations and pitch contour, predicting upsampled raw audio through transposed convolutional layers (HiFi-GAN multi-receptive field fusion).
* **The Discriminators ($D$):** 
  * Multi-Period Discriminator (MPD): Analyzes periodic harmonic patterns.
  * Multi-Scale Discriminator (MSD): Evaluates consecutive temporal windows.
* **Training Dynamics on Tesla T4 GPU:**
  * **100 Epochs / 2,100 Gradient Steps** completed in **16 minutes 42 seconds**.
  * Checkpoints saved every 10 epochs.

### Step 4: FAISS Feature Index Construction
Once training completes, Applio collects all ContentVec feature vectors extracted from our team member's audio and builds an inverted file index using **FAISS (Facebook AI Similarity Search)**:
* **Output:** `added_IVF256_Flat_Fast_teammate01_rvc_v2.index`.
* **Role:** Acts as a high-speed vector database of our team member's unique acoustic timbre.

## 4. Pipeline 2: The Applio Voice Conversion Inference Workflow

```
[Source Audio: stage1_base_speech.wav (Edge-TTS en-IN)]
                         │
                         ▼
        [1. Real-Time Feature Extraction]
        ├── ContentVec -> Source Content Vectors
        └── RMVPE      -> Source F0 Pitch Contour
                         │
                         ▼
        [2. FAISS Nearest-Neighbor Retrieval]
        Queries teammate01_rvc.index:
        Finds target speaker's closest matching acoustic clusters
                         │
                         ▼
        [3. Weighted Feature Blending]
        e_final = 0.80 * e_target (retrieved) + 0.20 * e_source
                         │
                         ▼
        [4. VITS Waveform Synthesis]
        Transposed convolutions in teammate01_rvc.pth
        reconstruct 40 kHz waveform with target timbre
                         │
                         ▼
   [Final Converted Deepfake: rvc_sample_001.wav]
   (Indian English Cadence + Team Member's Voice Identity)
```

### Step 1: Input Source Audio
* In our two-stage pipeline, the input is **`stage1_base_speech.wav`**, synthesized by Microsoft Edge-TTS (`en-IN-PrabhatNeural`).
* It contains pristine Indian English pronunciation and natural timing, but has a generic voice identity.

### Step 2: Real-Time ContentVec & RMVPE Extraction
* Applio extracts the phonetic sequence from the source audio using ContentVec.
* Applio tracks the source pitch contour $F_0$ using RMVPE.
* *Pitch Shift Parameter:* Set to `0` semitones (since both the base TTS and our team member share a similar speaking register).

### Step 3: FAISS Feature Retrieval & Blending (The "R" in RVC)
* This is the core innovation of RVC:
  * For each phonetic frame in the source speech, Applio queries the FAISS index for the $k$-nearest neighbor feature vectors actually spoken by our team member.
  * It blends the retrieved target vector with the source vector using the **Index Rate ($\alpha = 0.80$)**:
    $$\mathbf{e}_{\text{blended}} = 0.80 \cdot \mathbf{e}_{\text{target}} + 0.20 \cdot \mathbf{e}_{\text{source}}$$
  * *Why this matters:* The retrieved vectors inject our team member's true vocal tract formants into the speech, while the 0.20 source weight preserves subtle phonetic articulation.

### Step 4: VITS Generator Waveform Synthesis
* The blended feature representations and RMVPE pitch contours are fed into the trained generator (`teammate01_rvc_100e_2100s.pth`).
* Transposed 1D convolutional layers upsample the latent features by factors of $8 \times 8 \times 2 \times 2$, reconstructing the final 40,000 samples/second audio.

### Step 5: Output Audio
* **Result:** `rvc_sample_001.wav` — speech that articulates the input text with authentic Indian English rhythm, but carrying our team member's recognizable voice timbre.

## 5. Why Applio Was Essential (The Engineering Problem Solved)

When presenting to your guide or review panel, use this table to demonstrate your software engineering problem-solving:

| Technical Challenge | What Happened in Legacy RVC (2023) | How Applio Solved It |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Python Compatibility** | Hard-locked to Python 3.9/3.10; crashed on Colab's Python 3.12/3.13. | Modernized codebase running cleanly across **Python 3.10 to 3.12+**. |
| **`fairseq` Dependency** | Required compiling legacy C-extensions; failed with `_ARRAY_API not found` and wheel build errors. | **Completely removed `fairseq`**; uses native HuggingFace ContentVec loaders. |
| **Pitch Estimation** | Relied on heavy Crepe or noisy Harvest. | Integrated **RMVPE**, cutting extraction time by 70% and preventing octave leaps. |
| **Interface Flexibility** | Monolithic Gradio UI; difficult to automate. | Modular **`core.py` CLI** allowing headless scripted batch processing. |
| **Storage Persistence** | Files wiped on Colab disconnect. | **Google Drive Auto-Sync** automatically mirrored checkpoints every 10 epochs. |

```

           │
           ▼

• MFCC (26 dims) -> Vocal tract spectral envelope
• LFCC (26 dims) -> Catches HiFi-GAN high-frequency vocoder artifacts (>4 kHz)
• CQCC (26 dims) -> Catches RMVPE harmonic pitch micro-discontinuities
           │
           ▼
[Our ResNeXt CNN Classifier: models/deep_model.h5]

           │
           ▼

```

Applio acts as our **adversarial stimulus generator**. By generating high-quality synthetic speech from our own team member's voice, we proved that our detector's spectral features (**LFCC** and **CQCC**) inspect frequency-domain vocoder footprints that human ears cannot perceive.
