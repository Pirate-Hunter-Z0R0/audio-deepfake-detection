How It Works

Five-stage spectral analysis pipeline running end-to-end on your audio

Step 01
🔊
Resample & Normalize

Audio is resampled to 16 kHz mono and amplitude-normalized to [-1, 1] to eliminate recording volume bias.

Step 02
✂️
Silence Trimming

Leading/trailing silence is removed using energy-based VAD (top_db=20) so features capture only active speech.

Step 03
📡
MFCC + LFCC + CQCC

Three complementary spectral features capture vocal tract envelope, high-frequency artifacts, and pitch harmonics.

Step 04
📐
78-Dim Feature Vector

Mean + std statistics across 13 MFCC + 13 LFCC + 13 CQCC coefficients produce a 78-dimensional fingerprint.

Step 05
🤖
SVM Classification

A trained Support Vector Machine (RBF kernel) separates real vs. fake audio in high-dimensional feature space at 99.1% accuracy.
