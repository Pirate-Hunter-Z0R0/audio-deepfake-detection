Anti-Spoofing Architecture & Detection Methodology

Designed to counter advanced generative speech pipelines including Tacotron2, HiFi-GAN, and modern Retrieval-based Voice Conversion (RVC v2).

⚡

78-Dimensional Multi-Domain Feature Fusion

Captures complementary acoustic properties by combining 13 MFCCs (timbral envelope), 13 LFCCs (linear high-frequency vocoder cues), and 13 CQCCs (geometric harmonic stability), summarized via temporal mean and standard deviation.

🧠

Grouped Convolutions (ResNeXt)

Converts 1D features into a 6×13 2D spectral matrix. Exploits multi-branch cardinality paths to learn non-linear spatial relationships across cepstral coefficients without explosive parameter overhead.

🛡️

Cross-Vocoder Generalization Resilience

Evaluated across both legacy 2020 neural vocoders (FoR benchmark) and state-of-the-art 40kHz HiFi-GAN RVC voice clones with FAISS feature retrieval to quantify and defend against out-of-distribution attacks.
