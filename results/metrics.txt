EQUAL ERROR RATE (EER) EXPLANATION FOR VIVA:
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Equal Error Rate (EER) is the operating point on the ROC curve where
False Acceptance Rate (FAR - falsely passing a deepfake voice as real)
equals False Rejection Rate (FRR - falsely rejecting a real voice as fake).
A lower EER value indicates a superior and more reliable detector.
